The Amazon Fire TV Stick is the most common way our customers watch, and it is
also the quickest device to set up. This guide walks through the whole process.

## Before you start

You will need three things: your Fire TV Stick connected and updated, the
activation email we send after your payment is confirmed, and about five
minutes. Nothing else — there is no extra hardware to buy and no engineer visit.

Your activation email contains either a set of Xtream Codes details (a server
address, a username and a password) or a single M3U link. Either works.

## Allow apps from unknown sources

Fire TV blocks installs from outside the Amazon Appstore until you say
otherwise. This is a normal setting, not a workaround.

Open **Settings**, then **My Fire TV**, then **Developer options**, then
**Install unknown apps**. Turn the switch on for Downloader.

If you do not see Developer options, go to **Settings → My Fire TV → About** and
click **Fire TV Stick** seven times. The menu appears after that.

## Install the player

Search the Amazon Appstore for **Downloader** by AFTVnews and install it. Open
it once so it has permission to run, then enter the code from your welcome email
into its URL box and press Go.

When the download finishes, choose **Install**, then **Open**. You can delete
the installer file afterwards to free up space on the stick.

## Log in

Pick **Xtream Codes** if your email gave you a server, username and password.
Pick **Load M3U** if it gave you a single long link. Type the details exactly as
they appear, including the port number after the server address.

## If something does not work

The two most common problems both have quick fixes. If the app will not install,
the unknown-sources switch is usually still off for Downloader specifically
rather than for the device as a whole. If channels load but stutter, try a wired
connection or move the stick away from the back of the TV, where the metal
casing weakens Wi-Fi.

Still stuck? Message us on WhatsApp with your device model and we will walk you
through it.
